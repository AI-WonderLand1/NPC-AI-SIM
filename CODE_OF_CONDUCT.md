# Code of Conduct

AI WONDERLAND INNOVATION expects project collaboration to remain professional, respectful, and safe.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-managed community spaces for NPC-AI-SIM.

## Expected behavior

- Focus criticism on code, designs, data, and outcomes rather than people.
- Be patient with contributors who have different backgrounds or experience levels.
- Give actionable feedback and accept reasonable review feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, intimidate, or deliberately disrupt other participants.
- Follow the repository's security, contribution, and licensing requirements.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Report conduct or community concerns privately to **contact@dreammakerhub.website**.

For software vulnerabilities or security-sensitive reports, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting when available or email **security@dreammakerhub.website**. See `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment is not.
